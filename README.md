# Hemera

**Your entire life, organized one day at a time.**

Hemera (Ἡμέρα) takes its name from the Greek personification
of day.

Hemera is a thoughtful daily life planner that brings together
tasks, habits, events, routines, goals, and quick thoughts
in one place.

It helps people plan realistic days, adapt when plans change,
build consistency without guilt, and reflect on how they spend
their time.

## Product Principles

- Clarity over clutter.
- Consistency over streaks.
- Realistic planning over idealized schedules.
- Progress without guilt.
- Reflection over productivity scores.

## Status

Early product discovery. Requirements, technical architecture,
and MVP scope are not finalized yet.
